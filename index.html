CoPablo Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 17, 2026

CoPablo is an app for gym coaches. It lets a coach sign in, create or join a gym, keep a list of clients, write training plans, and set the gym’s weekly schedule. This policy describes what the app collects, how that information is used, and who it is shared with. It applies to the CoPablo Android app.

The developer of CoPablo is the data controller. Privacy questions and deletion requests go to rotem.mairon@gmail.com.

Information the app collects

Account. If you create an account with email, the app sends your email address and password to the sign-in service. The password is stored by that service so you can sign in again. The app itself does not keep a copy of the password. If you sign in with Google, Microsoft, or Apple, that provider authenticates you and sends the app your name, email address, and profile photo, when the provider supplies them. The app also stores which gym you last opened.

Gym. When you create or join a gym, the app stores the gym’s name, its join code, staff invitations, and your role (owner or coach).

Clients and training. Information a coach enters about a client is stored with that gym: name, phone number, sex, age band (which can show that the client is under 18, including under 13), body type, free-text notes, whether the client is checked in, and the client’s plan (exercise names, sets, repetitions, and weight). Notes are whatever the coach types. They can include health information, such as an injury. Programs the coach saves, custom exercises, and the weekly schedule (session name, day, time, length, number of seats, and notes) are stored the same way.

Contacts. Adding a client from the phone’s address book asks for permission to read contacts. While that picker is open, the app reads contact names, phone numbers, and photos on the device so you can search and choose one person. Only the person you choose is saved: their name, one phone number, and their photo. The rest of the address book is not uploaded and is not stored by us.

Photos. A photo taken from a contact is saved in the app’s storage on that phone. It is not uploaded.

On the phone. The app keeps a copy of the gym’s clients, plans, programs, and settings on the phone so it can open without a connection. A sign-in session is also stored on the phone.

The app does not take payments, show ads, or use an analytics or crash-reporting service. It does not collect your location or an advertising identifier.

How the information is used

  • To create your account and keep you signed in.
  • To show you the gyms you belong to, and to open the last one you used.
  • To keep the gym’s clients, plans, programs, and schedule, including a copy on your phone for offline use.
  • To let the owner and coaches of the same gym work from the same client list and schedule.
  • To fill in a new client from a contact you choose.

Who it is shared with

Staff of the same gym. The owner and coaches of a gym can see that gym’s clients, plans, programs, and schedule. They cannot see your other gyms, or the fact that you belong to another gym. A different gym cannot see this information.

Supabase. Supabase, Inc. hosts the database and the sign-in service. Account information and the gym data listed above are stored there so the app can sync. Supabase processes that information for CoPablo. Client photos are not included.

Sign-in providers. If you choose Google, Microsoft, or Apple, that company processes the sign-in and receives the fact that you used it to sign in to CoPablo. The app receives the name, email address, and profile photo that the provider returns.

CoPablo does not sell personal information and does not share it with advertisers or data brokers.

Children

CoPablo is for coaches. It is not directed at children, and a child should not create an account. A coach may record a client’s age, including an age under 13, as part of that client’s training record.

How long it is kept

Account and gym information is kept while the account and the gym are in use. Email rotem.mairon@gmail.com to ask for a copy of the personal information tied to your account, to correct it, or to delete your account.

Deleting the account removes the login and your membership in each gym. If you are the only person in a gym, that gym and its records are deleted with the account. If other staff still use the gym, the gym’s clients, plans, and schedule stay with them. If you are the only owner of a gym that still has other members, the gym has to be handed to another owner before the account can be deleted. Uninstalling the app removes the copy stored on that phone, including client photos.

Security

Gym data is available only to someone who is signed in as staff of that gym. No method of storage or transmission is perfectly secure.
